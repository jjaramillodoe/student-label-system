## Overview

The Intake Form (`/intake`) is for front-desk registration. It includes live duplicate detection (name **and** address), NYC address verification for new students, cabinet assignment, and immediate label printing.

## Student types

| Type | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| **NEW** | First-time registration at your program |
| **RETURNING** | Student already in the system — search by name/DOB, then record today's visit only |
| **CTE Orientation** | CTE orientation intake |
| **Other** | Special cases per school policy |

<Note>
  **Continuing Intake** was removed. Returning students use **RETURNING** on the same screen — no separate modal.
</Note>

## NEW student — step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Intake">
    Intake Members land on `/intake` after sign-in. Select **NEW**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter personal information">
    - First and last name (required)
    - **Date of birth** — a human-readable date appears beside the picker
    - **BE/ESL age rule** — students must be at least **21 years and 1 month** old for BE or ESL
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact and address">
    - Phone and email
    - **Paste full address** → **Parse**, or enter street, **Apt/Unit**, city, state, ZIP separately
    - **Verify with NYC Geoclient** before submit (when configured)
    - **View on Google Maps** after verify
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review duplicate matches">
    The system checks same DOB + similar name **and** compares home address:
    - **Same address** — strong signal; confirm it is not the same person
    - **Different address** — may be a sibling or someone who moved
    - Check **This is a different person** to flag siblings for Data Lead review
    - Use **Copy alert message** for Teams or email
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete intake details">
    Choose **BE** or **ESL**, intake session, activities, placement class, and time in/out as required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and print">
    On success, print the label with barcode (Label ID) and QR code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## RETURNING student — step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select RETURNING and search">
    Search by name and date of birth. Select the matching record from results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review visit history">
    An accordion shows prior intake visits (date, session, activities, times). Prior visit data is **not** overwritten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personal info and address are locked">
    Name, DOB, phone, email, and address fields are **read-only** for returning students. Only **today's visit** fields are editable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record today's visit">
    Enter fresh BE/ESL, session, activities, and time in/out for this visit. Submit appends a new visit snapshot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Address fields on intake

Street and apartment are stored separately:

- **Street** — e.g. `690 Grand St`
- **Apt/Unit** — e.g. `4B`

NYC Geoclient standardizes the **building** only; the apartment is kept in the `apt` field.

Details: [Address and NYC verification](/reference/address-and-verification)

## Returning archived students

When a previously archived student re-enrolls through Intake, the system reactivates their record and assigns the next open drawer automatically.
