## Overview

The Intake Form (`/intake`) is for front-desk registration. It includes live duplicate detection, cabinet assignment, and immediate label printing.

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Intake">
    Intake Members land on `/intake` after sign-in. Select the student type: **NEW**, **RETURNING**, **CTE Orientation**, **Continuing Intake**, or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter personal information">
    - First and last name (required)
    - **Date of birth** — a human-readable date appears beside the picker so staff can catch typos
    - **BE/ESL age rule** — students must be at least **21 years and 1 month** old for BE or ESL. If too young, the form shows how many days until they can register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review duplicate matches">
    For NEW students, the system fuzzy-matches same DOB + similar name. If a match appears:
    - Stop if it is the same person
    - Check **This is a different person** to flag a sibling for Data Lead review
    - Use **Copy alert message** to notify your Data Lead in Teams or email
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete intake details">
    Choose **BE** or **ESL**, intake session, activities, placement class, and time in/out as required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and print">
    On success, print the label with barcode (Label ID) and QR code. The QR opens the public student page — no login required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Returning archived students

When a previously archived student re-enrolls through Intake, the system reactivates their record and assigns the next open drawer automatically.
