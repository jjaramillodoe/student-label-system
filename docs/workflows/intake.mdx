## Overview

The Intake Form (`/intake`) is the front-desk registration screen. It supports live duplicate detection (name **and** address), NYC address verification for new students, session-hour validation, cabinet or archive-box location, and a clear success summary after save.

## Intake member guide + Print / PDF

At the top of Register Student, expand **Intake member guide** for NEW and RETURNING steps plus a pre-submit checklist. Use **Print / PDF** to save a desk copy (browser print → Save as PDF).

## Translate for multilingual students

Many students are more comfortable in another language. Use the **Translate** control at the top right of Intake:

1. Open the language dropdown (language icon).
2. Choose the student&apos;s language (Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, French, and many more).
3. Turn the screen toward the student so they can follow labels and options.
4. Set Translate back to **English** before the next student.

Staff still enter names and answers in English as the student tells them — Translate helps the student understand the form; it does not replace interpretation for complex cases.

## Student types

| Type | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| **NEW** | First-time registration at your program |
| **RETURNING** | Student already in the system — search by name/DOB, then record today's visit only |
| **CTE Orientation** | CTE orientation intake |
| **Other** | Special cases per school policy (age rule does not block BE/ESL the same way) |

<Note>
  **Continuing Intake** was removed. Returning students use **RETURNING** on the same screen — no separate modal.
</Note>

## NEW student — step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Intake and select NEW">
    Choose **NEW** under Student Status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check ASISTS (required gate)">
    Search by name, DOB (MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD), or both. A **DOB-only** search returns only students born that day.
    - Live match is the person with you → **Same person sitting here — log returning**
    - Match is a sibling / different person → **Not the same person sitting here** (unlocks NEW; flagged for Data Lead)
    - No match → **I checked ASISTS — this student was not found** so personal info unlocks
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter personal information">
    - First and last name (required) — A–Z, spaces, and hyphens
    - **Date of birth** — calendar min **1920-01-01**, max today; a human-readable date appears under the picker
    - **Age rules** — must be at least **16** for adult education; BE/ESL requires **21** on their birthday, or within **6 weeks** of turning 21 (near-eligible — intake may continue with an eligibility notice)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact and address">
    - Phone and email
    - **Paste full address** → **Parse**, or enter street, **Apt/Unit**, city, state, ZIP separately
    - **Verify with NYC Geoclient** before submit (when configured)
    - **View on Google Maps** after verify
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review duplicate matches">
    After name and DOB are entered, the panel under Date of Birth lists possible matches with **% match** and **Same DOB**. Same calendar DOB always surfaces for sibling review. See [Duplicates and siblings](/workflows/duplicates-and-siblings). Only use **This is a different person** for a true sibling or coincidence. Use **Copy alert message** or **Email with alert** to notify your Data Lead with a structured review note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete intake details">
    Choose **BE** or **ESL**, intake activities, placement class, **intake session**, Time In, and Leaving/Staying (Time Out required if Leaving).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Register Student**. The success screen shows a summary of what was saved — not a label print preview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Session hours (required)

Each school configures intake sessions with start and end times (for example **MORNING 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM** or **BUSHWICK EVENING 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM**).

- **Time In** and **Time Out** must fall inside the selected session window
- Invalid times show a red error under the field and **block submit**
- The allowed window appears under Time In once a session is selected

<Warning>
  Do not pick a morning session and enter an evening Time In. Fix the session or the time before saving — enrollment will also flag visits that fall outside session hours.
</Warning>

## RETURNING student — step by step

<Frame caption="RETURNING student selected — prior visit history and locked profile fields">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/intake-returning.png" alt="Returning student intake with visit history and locked personal information" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select RETURNING and search">
    Search by name and date of birth. Select the matching record from results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review visit history">
    An accordion shows prior intake visits (date, session, activities, times). Prior visit data is **not** overwritten.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personal info and address are locked">
    Name, DOB, phone, email, and address fields are **read-only**. Only **today's visit** fields are editable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record today's visit">
    Enter fresh BE/ESL, session, activities, and time in/out. Submit appends a new visit snapshot. Click **Log Visit & Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Returning archived students

If the student file is **already archived**, Intake does **not** assign a new drawer for the school year.

| Situation | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Archive box on file | Box label, storage location, **QR code**, and **View archive box** link |
| Archived but no box recorded | Warning to contact your Data Lead — visit can still be logged |
| Active student with no cabinet | Next available drawer is assigned (true unassigned case only) |

Staff can scan the archive-box QR to find the physical file when paperwork needs to be added.

## Age eligibility (DOB)

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Birth year before **1920** | Inline error — needs administrative confirmation |
| Under **16** | Intake sections lock; cannot enroll |
| **21+** for BE/ESL | Proceed normally |
| Turning **21 within 6 weeks** | Near-eligible — complete intake; banner + **Export Eligibility Notice** |
| Turning 21 in more than 6 weeks | Sections lock; message shows when they can re-apply; refer to P2G if needed |

## Under 21 — Pathways to Graduation (P2G)

Students who are not yet eligible for BE or ESL (and outside the 6-week near-eligible window) can be referred to [Pathways to Graduation](https://p2g.nyc/contact/). Use **Copy message for email** on the success screen when the under-21 notice appears.

## Success screen

After save you see:

- Student name, ID, and status badges
- A summary of the visit or registration (session, times, activities, contact)
- File location (active drawer **or** archive box)
- Address with optional Google Maps link
- Under-21 P2G referral card when applicable

Buttons: **Log Another Visit** or **Register Another Student** — label printing is done later from the Dashboard / Print Queue via **Download Word Doc** (Letter, 100%), not one-by-one at intake.

## Fix intake time issues

The yellow banner at the top of Intake lists students with time problems (outside session hours or handoff Time Out issues).

<Frame caption="Fix intake issues dialog — correct session hours or handoff Time Out">
  <img src="/images/screenshots/intake-fix-dialog.png" alt="Fix intake issues dialog for a student" />
</Frame>

Use **Fix** to:

1. Correct **session hours** (change session and/or Time In / Time Out)
2. Fix **handoff** issues (early Time Out or missing final clock-out)

## Duplicate flag example

Match cards show **% match** and **Same DOB**. Only check **This is a different person** for a true sibling or coincidence — that flags the record for Data Lead review on [Duplicates and siblings](/workflows/duplicates-and-siblings).

Under the match list:

| Control | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy alert message** | Paste into Teams, Slack, or an email draft |
| **Email with alert** | Open the Data Lead’s mail app with subject + full alert body filled in |

The alert includes school, reporter, NEW student fields, match details, and a link to the Duplicates review page.
