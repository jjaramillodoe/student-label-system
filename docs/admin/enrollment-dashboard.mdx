---
title: Enrollment dashboard
description: Track registrations by staff member and time period.
---

**Admin → Enrollment** (`/admin/enrollment`) shows registration activity.

## Metrics

- Today, This Week, This Month, All Time
- Staff leaderboard with ranking and progress bars
- Daily trend chart

## Table filters

Filter by period, staff member, or student/staff name search. Each row shows who registered the student (name + email).
