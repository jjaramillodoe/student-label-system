## School year rollover

**Storage → School Year** (`/admin/school-year`) tracks:

- Current fiscal year setting (School Settings)
- Cabinets ready to archive vs. still open
- Pending box assignments

Set the active fiscal year under **Schools / School Settings** — Intake and new records use that year automatically.

## Archive a cabinet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review readiness">
    Check Cabinet Health and Unassigned Queue before archiving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Archive from Cabinets">
    Choose box size (**50 / 100 / 200** files), storage location, and school year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Full closeout vs partial archive">
    - **Full end-of-year** — turn on **End-of-year closeout** if drawers are not completely full; the cabinet becomes Archived.
    - **Partial archive** — archive only selected statuses (e.g. Graduated / Transferred) and optionally specific drawers. The cabinet stays **Active** for remaining students.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview packing (required)">
    Click **Preview packing** to see who goes in which box. Override any student&apos;s box from the dropdown before you commit. Reset to auto packing if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Commit and print labels">
    Confirm the archive. Each box gets a unique ID. From the box label dialog: **Print Label** or **Download PDF**. Attach to the physical box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it later on the Archived tab">
    A full closeout moves the cabinet to **Storage → Cabinets → Archived**. Active cabinets stay on the **Active** tab (the default). Capacity totals ignore archived cabinets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store and retain">
    Keep physical files for the **7-year retention period** before disposal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Restore a cabinet

If a packed cabinet needs to accept new files again, open the **Archived** tab and click **Restore**. Confirm the prompt. The cabinet returns to **Active** (intake, moves, and the floor map can use it again). Box records and packing history are not deleted.

<Tip>
  If older archives still show students not linked to a box, use **Move student(s) to boxes** on the archived cabinet card before you restore.
</Tip>

## Public box pages

Box QR codes link to `/archive/box/{boxId}` — no login required. Staff and auditors can scan a box in storage to see location metadata and the full student list.

## Returning archived students at Intake

When an archived student returns for a visit, Intake shows the **existing archive box** (label, location, QR) instead of assigning a new active drawer. Staff add paperwork to the same physical file.

See [Intake workflow → Returning archived students](/workflows/intake#returning-archived-students).
