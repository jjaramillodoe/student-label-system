## Cabinets page

**Admin → Cabinets** (`/admin/cabinets`) manages where student files live.

- Create cabinets with multiple drawers and capacities
- Filter by school, capacity status, usage, and name
- **Smart Fill** creates standard cabinet structures quickly
- **Sync** recalculates drawer counts when data drifts

## Cabinet Health

**Admin → Cabinet Health** shows full, near-full, empty, and over-capacity drawers. Archived students with a valid archive box are excluded from "missing cabinet" warnings.

## Unassigned queue

**Admin → Unassigned** lists students missing a valid cabinet or drawer assignment.

## Bulk move

**Admin → Bulk Move** moves selected students with capacity validation before saving.

## Archived files

Archived students live in **archive boxes**, not active drawers. When they return through Intake, staff see the box location and QR instead of a new drawer assignment — see [Intake → Returning archived students](/workflows/intake#returning-archived-students).
