## 1. Sign in

Go to [student-label-system.vercel.app/auth/signin](https://student-label-system.vercel.app/auth/signin) and sign in with your NYC DOE account.

Your role determines which pages you can access:

| Role | Primary starting point |
| --- | --- |
| Intake Member | `/intake` |
| Data Member | Dashboard (`/`) |
| Data Lead | Dashboard + Admin tools |
| Admin | All schools + user management |

See [User roles](/user-roles) for the full permission matrix.

## 2. Complete your first task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Intake Member — register a student">
    1. Open **Intake** from the navigation.
    2. Enter name and date of birth — verify the human-readable date shown beside the picker.
    3. Choose **NEW** or **RETURNING** and complete BE/ESL fields.
    4. Submit and print the Avery label (barcode + QR).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data Member — find and print a label">
    1. Open the **Dashboard**.
    2. Search by name, Label ID, or Student ID.
    3. Select students and use **Print** for Avery 5163 sheets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Data Lead — review cabinet health">
    1. Open **Admin → Cabinet Health**.
    2. Resolve unassigned students or full drawers before intake peaks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Learn the ID system

Every student has two IDs:

- **Label ID** — short ID on the barcode and QR (e.g. `1979-JJ-0000001`)
- **Student ID** — demographic format for reporting (e.g. `JARAMILLOJAVIERR0119790522`)

Details: [Dual ID system](/reference/dual-id-system)
